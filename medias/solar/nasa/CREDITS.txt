Solar homepage - 2026-10-02

3D meshes and texture maps: NASA Visualization Technology Applications and
Development (VTAD). Mars: NASA/JPL-Caltech.
Original source pages and downloads: sources.json in this directory.
NASA media use guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

Adaptations: embedded maps resized/re-encoded as WebP, redundant glTF data
removed. Authored planetary meshes, UVs and Saturn rings are retained.
Solar surface shading, corona, prominence effects, lighting and composition
are artistic additions. Poster images are renders of this same scene.
No endorsement by NASA is implied.

Axial obliquities and orbital/rotation ordering:
https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/
The apparent sizes, distances and speeds are compressed for a blog header.
This is not a physical scale model or a real-time astronomical ephemeris.
All planets move independently; the Sun remains at a fixed location.
